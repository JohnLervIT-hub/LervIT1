# LervIT - Executive Summary
## The Uber of Moving | January 2026

---

## THE OPPORTUNITY

Calgary's $180M+ moving market is fragmented, opaque, and ripe for disruption. Customers face surprise bills and unreliable service. Freelance movers struggle with inconsistent work and payment delays.

---

## OUR SOLUTION

**LervIT** is an AI-powered two-sided marketplace connecting customers with verified freelance movers. Customers upload photos of their items and receive instant AI-generated quotes. They can track their mover in real-time and pay securely through the platform.

---

## KEY INNOVATION: AI VISION ENGINE

Our proprietary Vision Engine 2.0 is the **world's first photo-based moving quote system**:
- GPT-4o Vision identifies furniture from uploaded photos
- 3-layer processing: Vision → Database → Smart Corrections
- Outputs volume, vehicle type, mover count, and price in seconds
- **85%+ accuracy** on common household items

## BUSINESS MODEL

**15% platform commission** on every transaction
- Customer pays $200 for a move
- Mover receives $170 (85%)
- LervIT receives $30 (15%)

Uber-style destination charges ensure instant, automated payment splits.
