## 1. VIRAL HOOK FRAMEWORK

### Hook Formula
`HOOK = Pattern Interrupt + Emotion Trigger + Curiosity Gap`

### Hook Types (use one per piece)

**PAIN HOOKS** — Lead with audience frustration:
- "Tired of [pain point]?"
- "Stop [bad behavior] in Calgary"
- "Why your last move cost too much"
- "The real reason movers overcharge"

**SURPRISE HOOKS** — Lead with unexpected result:
- "I moved a whole apartment for $85 🤯"
- "Same-day movers in Calgary?!"
- "We moved a piano in 20 minutes"
- "[Price] for a full bedroom move?"

**CURIOSITY HOOKS** — Create information gap:
- "Nobody tells you this about moving..."
- "The moving trick Calgary landlords hate"
- "What movers don't want you to know"
- "This changed how Calgary moves"

**POV HOOKS** — Immersive perspective:
- "POV: Your mover shows up in 20 min"
- "POV: Getting an instant quote at 11pm"
- "POV: Moving day with no stress"
- "POV: Your stuff arrives in perfect condition"

**SOCIAL PROOF HOOKS** — Lead with credibility:
- "10,000+ Calgary moves later..."
- "5.0 stars. Every single time."
- "Calgary's most trusted movers"
- "Why 1,000 Calgarians chose LervIT"

**CONTROVERSY HOOKS** — Bold statement:
- "Hot take: moving companies are scams"
- "Unpopular opinion: moving is easy"
- "Traditional movers are overrated"
- "We said what nobody else would"

**LISTICLE HOOKS** — Promise specific value:
- "3 reasons your last move cost too much"
- "5 things to do before moving day"
- "2 ways to save $200 on your move"

**BEFORE/AFTER HOOKS** — Transformation:
- "Moving before LervIT vs after"
- "Old way vs LervIT way"
- "What $200 used to get you vs now"

## 2. PLATFORM-SPECIFIC RULES

### TikTok
- Hook: First 1 second (text overlay + visual)
- Format: 15–30 seconds optimal
- Style: Raw, authentic, fast-paced
- Text: Large captions, Gen-Z casual
- Sound: Trending audio when possible
- CTA: "Link in bio" or "Comment [word]"
- Caption: 1–3 lines max + 3–5 hashtags
- Best hooks: POV, surprise, controversy

### Instagram Reels
- Hook: First 3 seconds
- Format: 15–60 seconds
- Style: Polished but authentic
- Ratio: 9:16 mandatory
- Text: Captions ON always
- CTA: "Link in bio" or swipe
- Caption: 150 chars + hashtags
- Best hooks: Pain, before/after, social proof

### Facebook
- Hook: First 5 seconds
- Format: 30–90 seconds
- Style: Informative, community-feel
- Ratio: 16:9 or 9:16
- CTA: Direct link works
- Caption: Longer copy OK (2–3 paragraphs)
- Best hooks: Pain, listicle, social proof

### LinkedIn
- Hook: Lead with insight or data
- Format: Text-first, video secondary
- Style: Professional, thought leadership
- Tone: "We built this because..."
- CTA: "What's your experience?"
- Caption: 3–5 paragraphs with line breaks
- Best hooks: Controversy, listicle, data

## 3. HEYGEN SCRIPT OPTIMIZATION

### Script Structure (30 seconds)

```
0-3s:   HOOK (pattern interrupt)
3-8s:   PROBLEM (pain point)
8-18s:  SOLUTION (LervIT features)
18-25s: PROOF (social proof / result)
25-30s: CTA (one clear action)
```

### Script Rules
- Max 150 words for 30 seconds
- Short sentences: 5–8 words each
- Pause markers: use "..." for natural pauses
- Emotion markers use audio tags:
  - `<warmly>` for greetings/closings
  - `<confidently>` for facts/proof
  - `<empathetically>` for pain points
- Never say: "Certainly", "Of course", "Absolutely"
- Always say: real Calgary neighborhoods (Bridgeland, Beltline, Kensington, etc.)
- End with ONE clear CTA only

### Word-Per-Minute Guide
- Normal speech: 130 WPM
- 15 seconds = ~32 words
- 30 seconds = ~65 words
- 60 seconds = ~130 words

### Power Words for LervIT Scripts
- Instant, same-day, verified, trusted
- Calgary, local, community, neighbour
- Snap, book, track (brand tagline)
- Affordable, fair, transparent
- Stress-free, easy, fast, reliable

## 4. HIGGSFIELD PROMPT ENGINEERING

### Prompt Formula
`[SUBJECT] + [ACTION] + [ENVIRONMENT] + [STYLE] + [MOOD]`

### Proven Prompt Patterns

**Moving Scenario:**
> "Young Calgary couple carrying boxes into modern downtown apartment, golden hour lighting, cinematic, warm and hopeful mood, professional photography style"

**Mover Arrival:**
> "Friendly professional mover in uniform arriving at suburban Calgary home, smiling homeowner at door, sunny day, documentary style, authentic and trustworthy"

**City Context:**
> "Aerial view of Calgary skyline at sunset, moving truck on Crowchild Trail, cinematic drone shot, warm orange tones, 4K quality"

**Problem Scene:**
> "Stressed person surrounded by disorganized boxes in empty apartment, overwhelmed expression, realistic, relatable, urban Calgary setting"

**App Usage:**
> "Person using smartphone app to book moving service, modern minimalist setting, close-up of screen, clean and professional, lifestyle photography"

### Style Keywords That Work
- Cinematic, documentary, lifestyle
- Golden hour, soft natural light
- Warm tones, urban Calgary
- Authentic, real, unscripted
- Premium, clean, modern

### Keywords to Avoid
- Cartoon, illustrated, animated
- Text overlay (Higgsfield can't add text)
- Logos (add in post-production)
- Unrealistic scenarios

## 5. CALGARY-SPECIFIC CONTENT

### Neighbourhoods to Reference
- Downtown / Beltline (young professionals)
- Bridgeland (families, trendy)
- Kensington (boutique, community)
- Mahogany / Cranston (suburban families)
- Sunnyside (renters, young adults)
- Mission (upscale, professionals)

### Calgary Pain Points
- Extreme weather moves (-30 in winter)
- Condo elevator bookings
- Parking restrictions downtown
- Moving at end of month (busy season)
- Long distances between NE / SW

### Calgary Seasonal Hooks
- Spring: "Calgary moving season is here"
- Summer: "Beat the July 1st rush"
- Fall: "Moving before winter hits"
- Winter: "We move in -30° so you don't"

### Local Competitor Pain Points
- "$150/hr minimums elsewhere"
- "Hidden fees from big moving companies"
- "3-hour minimums for a single couch?"

## 6. CONTENT CALENDAR FRAMEWORK

### Weekly Cadence

```
Monday:    Educational (how-to, tips)

Wednesday: Behind-the-scenes / mover stories
Thursday:  Promotional (LERVIT10, offers)
```

- Pain point identification
- Brand introduction
- Goal: stop the scroll

**Week 2: EDUCATION**
- How LervIT works

- Goal: understand the product

**Week 3: CONVERSION**

- Offer / promo push
- CTA-forward content
- Goal: first booking

**Week 4: RETENTION**
- Community building
- Success stories
- Referral push
- Goal: repeat + word-of-mouth
